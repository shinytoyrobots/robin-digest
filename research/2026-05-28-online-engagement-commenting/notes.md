## Key Synthesis Points
1. Specificity is the universal authenticity signal across every platform.
2. Consistency > intensity; early exposures weighted most (Bornstein decelerating curve).
3. Reciprocity must be genuine; manufactured reciprocity is detectable by third parties (Jones 1964).
4. Sequence: consume → engage peripherally → familiarity → connect → only then ask.
5. DEBUNKED: "15x weight" (likely 8x, unconfirmed), "97% pod detection," "30-75x," "25x efficiency," "80/20 rule" = vendor folklore. SOLID: LinkedIn pod crackdown (VP Sachdeva), 621k-post dataset, Nielsen 90-9-1, 2022 Science weak-ties causal study.
6. Off-LinkedIn platforms (HN, Reddit, Mastodon, Bluesky, literary) reward depth/time-consistency and punish frequency + self-promotion far more strictly. "Lurk before you post" is universal.
